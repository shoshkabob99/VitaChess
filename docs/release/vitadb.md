# VitaDB listing

**Name:** Vita Chess

**Author:** shoshkabob

**Short description:**

A complete chess game: play the computer, solve puzzles, learn with 51 lessons, and collect trophies.

**Long description:**

Vita Chess is a complete chess game made for the PS Vita.

PLAY
- Play the computer at 8 difficulty levels, from Beginner (~400) to Master (~1800)
- Two-player pass-and-play, with optional auto-flip
- Chess clocks (3+2 up to 30 minutes), hints with arrows, undo, resign, and draw offers
- The computer plays real openings from a built-in book
- Tap any move in the move list, or use L and R, to look back at earlier positions

PUZZLES
- 1,000 tactics puzzles from the Lichess database, rated 400 to 2900
- Your own puzzle rating, with adaptive difficulty and streaks

LEARN
- 51 interactive lessons in 6 courses: The Basics, Checks and Checkmates, Tactics, Openings (seven guided walkthroughs), Strategy, and Endgame Practice with drills against the computer

STATS & TROPHIES
- Records against each level, win streaks, fastest win, favourite openings
- 25 trophies to unlock

LOOK AND FEEL
- Animated menu backgrounds, 7 board themes, and 5 piece sets
- Smooth piece animations, sound effects, and crisp text
- Touch and button controls

COMING NEXT
- Game review with blunders flagged, and an evaluation bar
- Ideas for later: more lessons, computer personalities and handicaps, a stronger computer, a PSP version, two-Vita multiplayer, and background music

The game auto-saves, and finished games are saved as PGN files in ux0:data/VitaChess/games/ for analysis on Lichess or chess.com.

Built with Lua Player Plus Vita by Rinnegatamante. Puzzles from the Lichess puzzle database (CC0). Open source under GPLv3.
